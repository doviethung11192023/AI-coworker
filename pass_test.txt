How should we rollout this leadership system to all regions?


The business problem we're solving is: Gucci Group leaders lack a shared mental model of our brand identity. We need to ensure all executives worldwide operate from the same 4 core values to maintain brand coherence.


What's the training plan we'll use? I want to make sure managers are ready for the rollout






Group DNA (brand DNA):
Gucci Group mission is to preserve luxury brand identity while scaling leadership consistency across maisons.
Our group culture requires leaders to balance creative autonomy with enterprise-wide standards.

Competency matrix (4x3 competency framework):
We will use a 4x3 competency matrix mapped to Vision, Entrepreneurship, Passion, Trust.

Vision: strategic narrative, long-term brand direction, cross-brand coherence
Entrepreneurship: ownership, speed of decision, innovation in local context
Passion: talent development, customer obsession, craft excellence
Trust: ethical leadership, collaboration, governance discipline
Role-family prioritization for alignment:
We will prioritize Brand Leaders, Regional Directors, and Functional Heads first because they drive cross-market execution and people decisions.

Stakeholder alignment concern and decision:
Main concern is over-standardization reducing local brand expression.
Decision: keep one Group-level matrix but allow local behavioral examples per brand/region.
